          At Chinese hawker stalls look for the sign 素食 (vegetarian), say no egg and ask about fish sauce. Sentosa, Mandai and Genting have thin veg options, so carry snacks there.

          Before you go

          • VisasIndian passports need a Singapore visa, applied through an authorised agent. Malaysia has allowed Indians in visa free for short stays through 2026; confirm when you book.
          • Arrival cardsSG Arrival Card (ICA site or MyICA app) and the Malaysia Digital Arrival Card, each within 3 days before landing. Both free.
          • Tax back in SingaporeAsk stores for an eTRS GST refund when you cross the minimum spend, then claim at Changi before security.
          • Getting aroundSingapore: tap a contactless Visa or Mastercard on MRT and buses. KL: Grab for most trips, MRT and monorail around the centre.
          • Book aheadSkyPark deck after 4 pm (timed entry), Night Safari, Cloud Forest combo, Genting SkyWorlds and the cable car on weekends.
          • WeatherNovember to December brings short afternoon showers in both countries. Keep the malls, Jewel and Skytropolis as rain plans.
          • BargainingBugis Street: a little. Petaling Street: a lot, start near half the asking price. Malls: never.
          • ElectronicsBuy at Apple stores, Mustafa or big chains. Travel forums keep warning tourists off pushy shops at Sim Lim Square.
